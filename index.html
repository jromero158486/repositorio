I am a computational health undergraduate thesis researcher at the LINC Lab at MIT–Harvard University, advised by Dr. Anastasia Yendiki, where I apply deep learning to neuroimaging and high-resolution connectomics.

Before that, I did research internships at Cornell University, UC Davis, FH Upper Austria, University of Waterloo, Google Explore LATAM, and Memorial University of Newfoundland. My main research interests are large language models in healthcare, machine learning, biomedical signal processing, and human–computer interaction. My most recent work explores AI agents in biosignal processing for continuous, context-aware recognition.
